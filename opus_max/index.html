How to Play

Controls

  • Mouse ←→ AD move
  • Click Space launch · fire lasers
  • Right-click E Shift unleash Pulse
  • Esc P pause · M mute
  • Touch: drag to move, tap to launch
  • Gamepad: stick move · A launch · X/RB pulse

The Pulse

Smashing bricks charges the PULSE meter. When it's full, unleash a shockwave that shreds bricks above your paddle, swats away enemy fire and rescues falling balls.

Chains

Break bricks in quick succession to build a chain. Every 5 bricks raises your multiplier, up to ×8. Every brick plays a note — chains climb the scale.
