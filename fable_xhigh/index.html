Lanternwake

Wake the lanterns. Carry the spark. Bring back the dawn.

 

Mouse, touch or ← → to steer  ·  Click or Space to launch and Flare  ·  P pause  ·  M mute  ·  F fullscreen

How to Play

SteerYour boat follows the mouse (or your finger, or the ← → keys). Keep the spark out of the river.
Launch & FlareClick or press Space to launch. Click again at the instant a spark lands on the boat for a Flare: a faster shot that pierces lanterns.
ChainWake lanterns in quick succession. The chain multiplies everything you score, up to ×8.
FirefliesCatch them with the boat. Forty fill the jar and earn a spare spark.
WishesAfter every stage, choose one wish. It stays with you until dawn.
GuardiansEach night ends with a guardian. Dodge what they throw; your spark does the rest.
