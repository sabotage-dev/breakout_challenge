How to play

The idea

Every brick you break sings a note. Chain hits without pause to climb the scale, raise your combo multiplier and fill the Resonance meter.

When it is full, unleash a Crescendo: for eight seconds every ball is unstoppable, the floor is sealed, steel shatters and points are doubled.

After each stage, pick one of three upgrades. Every fourth stage is a boss.
