A brick-breaking descent

HADAL

Full fathom five thy father lies;
Of his bones are coral made;
Those are pearls that were his eyes.— The Tempest

Mouse · Keyboard · Touch — best with sound on

How to play

Bounce the pearl. Break the reef. Sink through all five zones of the ocean to the floor of the Hadal trench, 10,935 metres down.

Steer

Move the mouse, press ← → or A D, or drag a finger. Click or Space launches the pearl.

Pulse

Click (or Space) the instant the pearl lands on your hull. A pulsed pearl hits harder, punches straight through a brick, and keeps your chain alive.

Focus

Hold Shift or the right mouse button to slow time while your sonar charge lasts. Your hull keeps its full speed.

Chain

Break bricks in quick succession and the multiplier climbs. Let the chain timer run dry and it starts over.

Upgrade

Clear a stage and pick one of three upgrades. They stack, and they last for the whole dive.

Survive

Drop your last pearl and you lose a life. Lose them all and the deep keeps you — though you may retry from the top of the zone.
