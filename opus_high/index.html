HOW TO PLAY

CONTROLS

Mouse / Touch — slide the paddle
← → / A D — keyboard movement
Click / Space — launch & release
P / Esc — pause   M — mute

BRICKS

  • Crystal — one hit
  • Armoured — two or three hits
  • Steel — unbreakable
  • Volatile — detonates neighbours
  • Prism — always drops a power-up

SCORING

Every brick broken before the ball returns to your paddle builds a combo. Each six links raise the multiplier — and the notes climb the scale. Clear a sector without losing a ball for a flawless bonus.

POWER-UPS

  • EExpand wider paddle
  • MMultiball every ball splits in three
  • LLasers twin auto-cannons
  • CMagnet catch & aim the ball
  • SSlow-mo calmer ball speed
  • FFireball melts through bricks
  • BBarrier saves one lost ball
  • ZChain Zap lightning arcs
  • 1UPExtra life
  • !Shrink avoid this one
