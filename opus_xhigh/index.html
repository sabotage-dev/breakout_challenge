The Rules of Light

Where the ball strikes the paddle sets its angle: edges send it wide. Clear every breakable brick to finish a stage. Each sector ends with a Guardian that fights back and stuns your paddle.

Hit bricks without touching the paddle to build a combo. Every 6 hits raise the multiplier (up to ×8), and each hit plays the next note of the soundtrack's chord, so long combos turn into melodies.

Breaking bricks fills the Overdrive meter. Unleash it for a shockwave, 8 seconds of piercing rainbow balls and double score.
